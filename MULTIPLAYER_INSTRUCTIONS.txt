================================================================================
  ONLINE MULTIPLAYER - MONSTER HUNTER 3 ULTIMATE (PC)
================================================================================

(La versión en español de este archivo es INSTRUCCIONES_MULTIJUGADOR.txt)

Up to 4 hunters can play. One person is the HOST and the others join them as
CLIENTS.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START (all players)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Install Radmin VPN (free, no sign-up) on every PC.
2. One of you creates a network in Radmin VPN and the others join it with its name and password.
3. Check that everybody shows as connected (in green) in that network.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IF YOU ARE THE HOST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the Launcher and go to the "Online Mode" tab.
2. Turn on the switch "Enable Online Multiplayer Mode".
   Windows will ask for administrator permission once: accept.
3. Choose "Create my room (Host)".
4. In "Your IP for friends" choose the Radmin VPN IP (it starts with 26.)
   and press "Copy IP". Send it to your friends.
5. Go to the "Main" tab and press PLAY.
   (The server starts by itself and what you typed is saved by itself.
   If Windows could block your friends, the Launcher tells you and
   fixes it with an administrator permission.)
6. In the game, talk to the Boatman (Neko) in Moga Village and choose:
   "Tanzia Port" -> "Network mode". Create your room and wait for your friends.

IMPORTANT: do not close the Launcher while you play.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IF YOU JOIN A FRIEND (CLIENT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Join your friend's Radmin VPN network.
2. Open the Launcher and go to the "Online Mode" tab.
3. Turn on the switch "Enable Online Multiplayer Mode" and accept the
   administrator permission.
4. Choose "Join a friend's room (Client)".
5. Paste the IP your friend gave you in "Host / Friend IP".
6. Press "Test Connection". If it turns green, your friend's server answers.
7. Your friend (the host) must have the game open BEFORE you press
   PLAY. Go to the "Main" tab and press PLAY (what you typed is saved by itself).
8. In the game, talk to the Boatman (Neko) and choose:
   "Tanzia Port" -> "Network mode". Look for your friend's room and join it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING DOES NOT WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• "Test Connection" turns red:
  - Check that both of you are connected to the same Radmin VPN network.
  - Check that the IP is typed correctly (the host's, it starts with 26.).
  - Ask the host to open the game first.

• You cannot see each other's room (or you get disconnected when you enter it):
  - Press "Network Diagnostics" in the Online Mode tab on both PCs: it says
    what is missing. If the firewall is wrong, press PLAY and accept to repair it.
  - If a Windows window asks for permission, never press "Cancel": it creates a
    block that overrides everything else.

• The Launcher did not ask for permission when you turned the switch on:
  - Turn the Online Mode switch off and on again.

• The audio cuts or the FPS drop when you are the host:
  - Close other heavy programs. The host runs the game and the server at the
    same time, so it needs the most powerful PC of the group.
